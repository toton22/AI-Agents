Pick a track

Everyone does the thirteen modules. The tracks differ in which build methods you practise on and how deep you go into code. You can switch later. Plenty of managers start on the first track and drift into the third once they've seen how short an agent loop really is.

Manager's lens

Even on Track A, do the raw-API page once, slowly, reading the plain-English notes. Seeing that an "agent" is a loop of model call, tool call and check removes most of the mystique, and makes you much harder to oversell in a vendor meeting.

Skills by level

Level 1 · Foundations (modules 01–04)

  • Explain the difference between a chatbot, a copilot, a workflow and an agent, with a business example of each
  • Estimate the monthly token cost of an agent from volume and prompt size
  • Write a system prompt with role, rules, escalation and output format
  • Describe a tool in a schema and say which tools need human approval

Level 2 · Building (modules 05–09)

  • Ground an agent in company documents and make it cite sources
  • Decide what an agent should remember, for how long, and how a customer can make it forget
  • Choose the simplest pattern that solves a problem, and say when multi-agent is worth the cost
  • Connect an agent to company tools through MCP

Level 3 · Corporate (modules 10–13)

  • Build a golden test set and report pass rates before and after a change
  • Threat-model an agent and design layered guardrails
  • Draw the production architecture and plan a 30-60-90 day pilot
  • Write the business case, the KPI sheet and the governance entry for an agent

Suggested assessment split

Quizzes 15%, lab submissions 20%, mid-term 15%, capstone 50% (use the rubric). Adjust to your programme's rules.

Four-week sprint for working managers

For executive education or a team learning together at work. About four hours a week.

  1. Week 1 · Understand

    M01, M02 and the concept map. Run the Agent Lab. List ten repetitive decisions in your own team.

  2. Week 2 · Prototype

    M03 and M05. Build one of your ten as a configured assistant or in Copilot Studio. Test it on twenty real past cases.

  3. Week 3 · De-risk

    M10 and M11. Write a golden test set, red-team the prototype, decide where a human must approve.

  4. Week 4 · Decide

    M12 and M13. Fill in the business-case template, pick a build method for production, and take a pilot proposal to your leadership.
